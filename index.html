Learn Model Risk with Phoebe

Brackwater submitted a credit scorecard with a development AUC of 0.863 and a recommendation to deploy. The number was correct and it reproduced every time the query ran. One of its eleven features is logged after the outcome window opens; without it the model scores 0.772, and on the following year's applications it scores 0.682, six thousandths ahead of a benchmark built from three columns. Nothing in that story is a modelling error, and all of it is what the person who signs the approval is accountable for. Six leader sessions on challenge, materiality and sign-off, ten practitioner sessions on how a validation is actually run, and a bench where the truth is known by construction so your findings can be checked rather than argued about.

What is real here

Nothing on the bench is a simulation of a result.

The applications6,000 from a structural model written out in assets/mr-live.js, fixed seeds, every coefficient visible
Every modelA real logistic regression fitted in the page by iteratively reweighted least squares, on click
Every AUCA rank-based calculation over the scores those regressions produced. Nothing is hard-coded
